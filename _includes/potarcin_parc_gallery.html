Rule (t10). Every cell has a 180-degree partner: the cell at the opposite position through the grid's centre. For each cell, keep its own colour if it is non-black, otherwise take the colour of its partner. In these puzzles at most one of any partner pair is non-black to begin with, so each pair ends up filled with that single colour on both sides.
